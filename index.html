HOOKPUNKS

PROTOTYPE BUILD 1.0 — SOLAR CITY ONE

You're a courier, and you're already on the job — holding the back of a car doing eighty down the highway. Slide around it, let go when you like, and take the road from there.

  • SHIFT unattached breaks traction for a drift — the board swings ahead of your line for a tighter corner at speed
  • On a big air, W/S flip the body and A/D spin it — combine them for McTwists. Flips need more than an ollie's worth of clearance, so a flat pop won't do. Land upright or you bail
  • The camera holds its line while you spin, so you can land backwards — C turns the rider round with the board and your speed untouched
  • Ollie onto a lane-divider rail to grind — land square for a 50-50, side-on for a boardslide. It builds speed the longer you hold, and SPACE pops you off
  • WASD skate  ·  SPACE ollie  ·  MOUSE + CLICK fire the harpoon, click again to release
  • On the rope: W/S reel in and out  ·  SHIFT drags the tail and A/D strafe you across behind the car  ·  Q release. Reeling builds swing, and swing is the slingshot
  • Hitched, she draws a sidearm in her free hand and tracks the mouse with it. F or Q to let go, SPACE to kick off
  • HITCH JUMP: from a car, fire the harpoon at another one within half the usual range and the winch drags you across — hook a flank and you arrive on the flank
  • On the rope, W reels in and S both pays out slack and brakes — drag your foot and you fall back to the end of the line, which is how you set up a swing
  • The harpoon also bites structures — anchor an overpass deck, pillar or tower and swing beneath it. Reeling in shortens the pendulum and spins you up
  • You start hitched. A/D slides you around the car's flanks and rear, SPACE kicks you off, F lets go. To hitch again, get alongside any car and match its pace
  • Crossings at s262 and s400 pass overhead — skate under them, or harpoon the underside and swing
  • Crossings pass over the highway — skate under them, or hit kicker-D at s232 above 83 km/h to land on one

Press ` for the debug menu — arrow keys adjust, and the key list lower-left toggles the probe grid, arc trail, landing prediction, collision bounds and rope wrapping.
